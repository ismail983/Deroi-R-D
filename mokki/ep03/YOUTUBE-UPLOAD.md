## Description
Splish, splash, it's bath time! 🛁🦆 Mokki played all day in the park and now he's dirty and sleepy. Papa Moko fills the tub with warm water, lots of bubbles and a rubber duck, and baby Pip joins too!

Sing along with Mokki and learn:
🌡️ A grown-up checks the water is warm, not too hot
🧽 Wash with soap from head to toe: face, ears, tummy, arms and feet
🫧 Rinse the bubbles away and pull the plug
🧺 Dry with a soft towel
❤️ Never play in the bath alone, always with a grown-up

A gentle bath-time and bedtime routine song for babies, toddlers and preschoolers (ages 0–5). 💚
