# Mokki's Family – Character Sheet

Drawn by `draw_family.py` in Mokki's exact style: scalloped body, thick outline, curly loop antenna with a bead,
round white eyes with navy pupils, pink cheeks, simple smile, flat pastel colours. No noses, ears or clothes.
Each file has a `-nomouth` twin for lip-sync; `family.json` stores eye/mouth positions for the animation rig.
