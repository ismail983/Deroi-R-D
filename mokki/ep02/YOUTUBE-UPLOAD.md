## Description
Splish, splash! 🧼💦 Mokki played in the garden with his little sister Pia and now his hands are muddy! Mama Mimi says tiny germs are hiding there… so let's wash our hands with soap and water!

Sing along with Mokki and learn:
💧 Turn on the water and wet your hands
🧼 Pump the soap and make bubbles
👐 Rub palms, backs of hands, between the fingers, thumbs and nails, while we count to 20!
🚿 Rinse the bubbles away
🧻 Dry with a towel
⏰ When? Before eating, after the potty, after playing outside, after sneezing or coughing

Made for babies, toddlers and preschoolers (ages 0–5). Meet Mokki's family too: Papa Moko, Mama Mimi, Pia and baby Pip! 💚
