# Episode 2 – Wash Your Hands Song 🧼

**Title:** Wash Your Hands Song 🧼 | Mokki & Friends | Nursery Rhymes & Kids Songs for Toddlers
**Cast:** Mokki (Teddy Twinkle voice), Mama Mimi (Libby voice), Pia (seen), Papa, Pip (seen at dinner)
**Lesson:** wash hands with soap and water: wet, soap, rub (palms, backs, between fingers, thumbs, nails) while counting to 20, rinse, dry.
When: before eating, after the potty, after playing outside, after sneezing or coughing.
