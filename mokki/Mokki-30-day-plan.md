# Mokki & Friends – 30-Day Upload Plan

**Format for every video**
- Length: 3–5 min. One Short (≤60 s) cut from the chorus.
- Structure: Mokki's hello (~17 s) → small problem in Mokki World (~26 s) → song with on-screen lyrics (90 s) → Mokki's lesson in numbered steps (~35 s) → sing-along: the song again, "Now YOU sing!" (90 s) → goodbye + "see you tomorrow!" (~18 s)
- Title format: `<Topic> Song <emoji> | Mokki & Friends | Nursery Rhymes & Kids Songs`
- Audience: Made for Kids ✅
- Every video = **channel intro (6 s)** + episode + **outro with Like & Subscribe (14 s)**, built by `common/branding.py` (`wrap_episode`). Keep the episode itself ≤ 4:40 so the whole video stays within 3–5 min.
- Note: YouTube turns off end screens and cards on Made for Kids videos, so the Like/Subscribe ask lives inside the video (the outro).

# Episode 1 – Brush Your Teeth Song 🪥

**Title:** Brush Your Teeth Song 🪥 | Mokki & Friends | Nursery Rhymes & Kids Songs
**Short title:** Brush with Mokki! 🪥 #nurseryrhymes #kidssongs #toddlers
**Length:** ~2:30

## Scene-by-scene script

**Scene 1 – Mokki's bedroom, morning (0:00–0:12)**
*Visual:* Sun peeks through a round window. Mokki pops up from a leaf-blanket bed, antenna boinging.
**MOKKI:** Hi friends! It's me, Mokki! Good morning! *(yawn)* Ooh, I just woke up… and my teeth feel fuzzy! Do your teeth feel fuzzy too? Let's brush them together! Get your toothbrush… ready? Let's sing!

**Scene 2 – The problem (0:12–0:30)**
*Visual:* Peanut the bird flies in holding a giant candy. Mokki's tummy rumbles.
**PEANUT:** Mokki! Mokki! Candy for breakfast?!
**MOKKI:** Hmm… candy makes little sugar bugs on my teeth! First, let's brush!

**Scene 3 – Song (0:30–2:00)** – *on-screen lyrics, bouncing toothbrush icon on each "brush"*
> **Verse 1**
> Wake up, wake up, the sun says hi,
> Mokki stretches to the sky!
> Grab your brush and toothpaste too,
> Tiny pea, just like I do!
>
> **Chorus**
> Brush, brush, brush your teeth,
> Top and bottom, up and down!
> Brush, brush, brush your teeth,
> Round and round and round and round!
>
> **Verse 2**
> Little circles, front and back,
> Bye-bye, bye-bye, sugar snack!
> Count with Mokki, one, two, three,
> Shiny teeth for you and me!
>
> *(Chorus)*
>
> **Outro**
> Swish and spit, and smile so bright,
> Morning teeth and teeth at night!

*Visual during song:* Lulu and Peanut join with their own brushes; cartoon sugar bugs wave bye-bye and float away.

**Scene 4 – What we learned (2:00–2:20)**
**MOKKI:** *(big sparkly smile)* Ding! Look how shiny! We brush in the morning and at night. Two times every day!
**LULU:** And just a tiny bit of toothpaste, like a little pea!

**Scene 5 – Goodbye (2:20–2:30)**
**MOKKI:** Bye-bye, friends! See you tomorrow in Mokki World! *(waves, antenna wiggles)*
